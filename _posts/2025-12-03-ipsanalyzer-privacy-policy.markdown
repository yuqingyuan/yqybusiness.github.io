This privacy policy applies to the IpsAnalyzer app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad Supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

**Information Collection and Use**

IpsAnalyzer is an iOS crash file analysis tool that helps you analyze and debug iOS crash reports (.crash files). The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

- Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
- The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
- The time spent on the Application
- The operating system you use on your mobile device
- Device information such as device model, device ID, and unique identifiers

The Application requires access to your device's file system to read iOS crash files (.crash files) and dSYM files for symbolication. All crash files and related data are processed locally on your device and are not automatically transmitted to external servers.

The Application may store your crash analysis history locally on your device to provide you with a better user experience. This history is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to external servers unless you explicitly choose to sync or backup your data.

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.

For a better experience, while using the Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to Device ID. The information that the Service Provider request will be retained by them and used as described in this privacy policy.

**Crash File Data**

When you use IpsAnalyzer to analyze iOS crash files, the Application processes crash reports locally on your device. Crash files may contain sensitive information including:
- Application names and bundle identifiers
- Stack traces and memory addresses
- Thread information and register states
- Exception types and error messages

All crash file analysis is performed locally on your device. Crash files and their contents are not automatically uploaded to external servers. The Application may store analysis results locally for your convenience, but you retain full control over this data and can delete it at any time.

If you choose to share crash analysis reports or export data, you are responsible for ensuring that any shared information does not contain sensitive or confidential data.
